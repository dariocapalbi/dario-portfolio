A global campaign celebrating music, movement and self-expression.

Do What Moves You is a global brand campaign that puts music, dance and individuality at the heart of BACARDÍ's identity. Built around the belief that everyone should feel free to express themselves, it encourages people to follow their passions, embrace their creativity and connect through the things that move them.

Rather than focusing solely on the product, BACARDÍ places itself within the culture surrounding it. Inspired by Caribbean rhythms, nightlife and contemporary music, the campaign captures the energy of spontaneous moments and shared experiences — bold imagery, dynamic movement and an expressive visual language.

It extends beyond traditional advertising into social media, music partnerships, live experiences and outdoor activations. By working with artists and building experiences around music and dance, the message turns into an invitation to participate.

The creative idea is simple: movement is a universal form of self-expression. Whether through music, dance or personal passions, Do What Moves You celebrates the freedom to be yourself.

For BACARDÍ it becomes more than a tagline. It becomes a way to connect the brand with the people, cultures and experiences that move its audience.
