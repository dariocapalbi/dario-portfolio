Online now

It was the fourth project of my UX/UI bootcamp at Ironhack, and it ended where projects like that usually end — a prototype and a presentation. I kept building it on my own once the bootcamp was over, now with AI. This is where that got to: not a picture of an app, an app. Every version runs, which is the only way to find out that a flow reads well on a board and badly in a hand.

SurveyTakes more than one answer, and what you pick sets your weekly target and chooses today's workout.
WorkoutPress play and the exercises run one after another, each with its own countdown.
CommunityFilters the way a meetup app really filters: date, distance, category, type, price, sort.
TrainerOpen a profile, read the reviews, send a message — and get an answer back.
ProgressEverything you do in here moves the week. Nothing is decoration.
