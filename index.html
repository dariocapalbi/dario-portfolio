About Me

I'm Dario, a creative UX/UI and graphic designer based in Barcelona, with more than twelve years of experience across design, digital and communication.

I started my career in Turin at Synapsy Design, working on projects for brands including Illy, Coca-Cola, Toshiba, Baileys and Kinder. I then moved to Seville to join Oklan, working across web, print and communication projects. Since 2015, I've also worked independently with international brands and agencies across the UK and Spain.

Alongside design, I spent almost eight years at the Consulate of Panama in London, where I coordinated a department, managed relationships with international companies, handled digital applications and trained new team members. It gave me a different perspective on people, processes and how things work behind the scenes.

My UX/UI journey started with Ironhack's bootcamp in London, where I developed my skills in user research, wireframing and prototyping, building on my background in digital design from IED Turin. Since then, I've continued to expand how I work with digital products, keeping up with AI tools, generative design and new approaches such as AI-assisted workflows and vibe coding. I'm also currently studying comics and illustration at Escola Joso.

I care about clarity, restraint and details that earn their place — no fluff, no filler, no design by committee.
